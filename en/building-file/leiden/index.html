Municipality of Leiden

Where is the building file of your house in Leiden?

Which archive holds the file depends on the year the permit was granted. Below is where to be for each period, with the date on which I checked it.

Period by period

The counters in Leiden

In Leiden the year 1929 is the first question. The city hall burned down that year and the old building permit archive went with it. For a nineteenth century house that is the whole answer: the file no longer exists.

Before 1929

Nothing survives from before 1929. It went up with the city hall.

No file survives from these years, so there is nothing to request.

1929 to 2010

Erfgoed Leiden en Omstreken

Held in the e-depot of Erfgoed Leiden, free to view and download through the visitor portal. For the permit application itself, or the correspondence inside a file, you need the reading room separately: studiezaal@erfgoedleiden.nl.

As I noted it in Dutch, with the links and the exact terms you will need on their website:

in het e-depot. Gratis te bekijken en te downloaden via het bezoekersportaal. https://www.erfgoedleiden.nl/collecties/bouwtekeningen

  • Voor de vergunningaanvraag zelf of de correspondentie in een dossier moet u apart bij de studiezaal zijn: studiezaal@erfgoedleiden.nl.

I can look this file up and download it for you.
