Municipality of Breda

Where is the building file of your house in Breda?

Which archive holds the file depends on the year the permit was granted. Below is where to be for each period, with the date on which I checked it.

This covers Breda, Prinsenbeek, Ulvenhout, Bavel, Teteringen, Princenhage and Ginneken as well.

Period by period

The counters in Breda

Breda is one of the few municipalities where almost the entire archive can be consulted without anyone in between. In 2025 some 850,000 files were put in a public portal.

Up to 2013

Stadsarchief Breda

Up to 2013 the Stadsarchief Breda holds the files, and they can be viewed directly online in its public portal, free of charge. Buildings with a security risk, such as police stations and the town hall, are screened off.

As I noted it in Dutch, with the links and the exact terms you will need on their website:

direct online in te zien via het publieksportaal https://archief.stadsarchiefbreda.nl/vergunningen. Kosteloos. Panden met een veiligheidsrisico, zoals politiebureaus en het stadhuis, zijn afgeschermd.

I can look this file up and download it for you.

From 2014 and permits still in progress

gemeente Breda

From 2014, and for permits still in progress, the municipality of Breda handles it through a digital form. It helps to have the case number at hand, in the form Z followed by the year and six digits; otherwise the street and house number.

As I noted it in Dutch, with the links and the exact terms you will need on their website:

met een digitaal formulier op https://www.breda.nl/bouwdossier-inzien. Handig om bij de hand te hebben: het zaaknummer in de vorm Z plus jaartal plus zes cijfers, anders straat en huisnummer.

For this period I have not yet checked whether the archive also supplies me.
