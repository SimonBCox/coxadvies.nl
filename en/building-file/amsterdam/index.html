Municipality of Amsterdam

Where is the building file of your house in Amsterdam?

Which archive holds the file depends on the year the permit was granted. Below is where to be for each period, with the date on which I checked it.

This covers stadsdelen Centrum, Noord, Oost, West, Nieuw-West, Zuid, Zuidoost and Weesp as well.

Period by period

The counters in Amsterdam

Two boundaries matter in Amsterdam, 1905 and 2010. The middle stretch is online and free to download, and almost the entire housing stock falls inside it. The city archive holds foundation information as well, which in Amsterdam is usually the first thing a structural question needs.

Before 1905

Stadsarchief Amsterdam

Search the image bank by address. The drawing downloads with the icon to the left of the image.

As I noted it in Dutch, with the links and the exact terms you will need on their website:

beeldbank. Zoek op adres; de tekening is te downloaden met het pictogram links naast de afbeelding. https://archief.amsterdam/beeldbank

1905 to 2010

Stadsarchief Amsterdam

Free to download from the city's own data map. Search by address or postcode, pick the right address and scroll down the row Adresgegevens to the heading Bouwdossiers. These files were built up by the city districts and handed over to the archive later, so one file often covers a whole block of houses. The municipality names foundation information here explicitly.

As I noted it in Dutch, with the links and the exact terms you will need on their website:

online in de datakaart van de gemeente. Zoek op adres of postcode, kies het juiste adres en blader in de rij Adresgegevens naar het kopje Bouwdossiers. https://data.amsterdam.nl/bouwdossiers

  • Deze dossiers zijn door de stadsdelen opgebouwd en daarna aan het Stadsarchief overgedragen. Eén dossier gaat vaak over een heel bouwblok.
  • De gemeente noemt hier uitdrukkelijk ook funderingsinformatie. Bij een constructievraag in Amsterdam is dat meestal het eerste wat u zoekt.

I can look this file up and download it for you.

From 2011

gemeente Amsterdam

For permits from 2011 onwards the city gives no online counter and no email address, only the phone number 14 020, Monday to Friday between 08.00 and 18.00. I have not established whether they will supply me on your behalf, so this is the one period here I cannot promise to request for you.

For this period I have not yet checked whether the archive also supplies me.

Worth knowing

Some buildings are closed off for security reasons. Those only open with the permission of the owner or another interested party.

As I noted it in Dutch, with the links and the exact terms you will need on their website:

Niet-openbaar verklaarde panden: een deel van de panden is om veiligheidsredenen afgeschermd en komt alleen los met toestemming van de eigenaar of belanghebbende.

Bronnen: https://www.amsterdam.nl/wonen-bouwen-verbouwen/bouwen-verbouwen/bouwtekeningen-bouwvergunningen-opvragen/, https://archief.amsterdam/bouwdossiers.

I checked these details on 14 September 2026. Counters and period boundaries change; if something no longer holds, please tell me.
