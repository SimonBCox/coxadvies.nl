Municipality of Amersfoort

Where is the building file of your house in Amersfoort?

Which archive holds the file depends on the year the permit was granted. Below is where to be for each period, with the date on which I checked it.

This covers Amersfoort, Hoogland and Hooglanderveen as well.

Period by period

The counters in Amersfoort

Amersfoort has one boundary, October 2010, and an obliging archive before it. Archief Eemland supplies digitally and free of charge, usually in about five working days.

1856 to October 2010

Archief Eemland

Requests go by email to archiefeemland@amersfoort.nl. Give the street and house number, and say explicitly what you are after: floor plans, structural drawings, the structural calculation, the original construction or the last permitted state. Processing takes about five working days and it costs nothing, digital copies included; a charge only applies to a file that has not been digitised and runs to more than five scans. The archive delivers a complete set per permit number. Files from 1979 to 2010 were scanned and the paper destroyed, so those are digital by definition.

As I noted it in Dutch, with the links and the exact terms you will need on their website:

Aanvragen per mail: archiefeemland@amersfoort.nl. Geef straat + huisnummer en zeg er expliciet bij wat u zoekt (plattegronden, constructietekeningen, constructieberekening, originele bouw of laatst vergunde toestand). Verwerkingstijd ongeveer vijf werkdagen. Kosteloos, ook digitaal; alleen bij een niet-gedigitaliseerd dossier met meer dan vijf scans worden kosten berekend. Tel. 033 469 5017, bezoek Het Eemhuis (3e verdieping), Eemplein 73. https://www.archiefeemland.nl/bronnen/bouwtekeningen/

  • Levert per vergunningnummer een complete set; oude fabrieksdossiers zitten er gewoon bij (2026DB13 kreeg 1967, 1989 en 2000 in één keer).
  • Dossiers 1979–2010 zijn retrospectief vervangen (gescand, papier vernietigd), dus die zijn per definitie digitaal.

From October 2010

gemeente Amersfoort

From October 2010 the permits department of the municipality handles it, through an information request to info@amersfoort.nl.
