Municipality of Eindhoven

Where is the building file of your house in Eindhoven?

Which archive holds the file depends on the year the permit was granted. Below is where to be for each period, with the date on which I checked it.

This covers Woensel, Strijp, Stratum, Gestel, Tongelre and Acht as well.

Period by period

The counters in Eindhoven

Eindhoven splits at 1989. Anything older sits with the regional archive and can simply be requested. Anything younger sits with the municipality, and the file of an Eindhoven house built before 1989 is the one most people are after.

Up to 1988

Regionaal Historisch Centrum Eindhoven

Requested with the form on the archive's own site, no login needed. The archive supplies the technical documents only: building drawings, structural drawings and calculations, and nothing else from the file. Reckon on about a fortnight for an answer. Their reading room opens on Tuesdays and Thursdays by appointment.

As I noted it in Dutch, with the links and the exact terms you will need on their website:

(RHCe), Raiffeisenstraat 18. Aanvragen met het formulier op https://www.rhc-eindhoven.nl/bouwdossiers-aanvragen, zonder inloggen. Tel. (040) 264 99 40, op werkdagen van 08:30 tot 17:00. Het archief verstrekt alleen de technische stukken: bouwtekeningen, constructietekeningen en berekeningen, en geen andere dossierstukken. Reactietijd ongeveer veertien dagen. De studiezaal is op dinsdag en donderdag open op afspraak.

I can request this file for you.

From 1989

gemeente Eindhoven

From 1989 the municipality supplies it, and its own counter asks for a Dutch DigiD or eHerkenning login, which I cannot do on your behalf. The municipality charges € 34 per file.

As I noted it in Dutch, with the links and the exact terms you will need on their website:

Alleen online in te zien na inloggen met DigiD of eHerkenning. Kosten € 34 per dossier, levering binnen vijf werkdagen via beveiligde mail; de factuur volgt ongeveer twee weken later. Wie geen van beide heeft, maakt een afspraak via vergunningen@eindhoven.nl.

This counter only supplies the owner in person, after logging in with a Dutch DigiD. I cannot do that for you.

Worth knowing

As I noted it in Dutch, with the links and the exact terms you will need on their website:

Het RHCe beheert de bouwdossiers van twintig gemeenten in Zuidoost-Brabant, waaronder Helmond, Veldhoven, Nuenen, Best, Geldrop-Mierlo, Deurne en Valkenswaard. De jaargrens verschilt per gemeente en staat per gemeente op de site van het archief.

Bronnen: https://www.eindhoven.nl/bouwen/vergunningen/bouwdossier-opvragen en https://www.rhc-eindhoven.nl/bouwdossiers.

I checked these details on 6 September 2026. Counters and period boundaries change; if something no longer holds, please tell me.
