Municipality of Amstelveen

Where is the building file of your house in Amstelveen?

Which archive holds the file depends on the year the permit was granted. Below is where to be for each period, with the date on which I checked it.

Period by period

The counters in Amstelveen

Amstelveen has one boundary, 1 October 2010. Everything before it was handed over to the Amsterdam city archive and is online there, with scans you can download. What was permitted after that sits with the municipality, behind a Dutch DigiD login.

Up to 30 September 2010

Stadsarchief Amsterdam

A century of Amstelveen building files sits digitally at the Amsterdam city archive, searchable and scanned. Single documents and whole files download from the buttons on the detail page, free of charge.

Some buildings are closed off for security reasons. Those only open with a signed statement of consent from the owner or another interested party.

As I noted it in Dutch, with the links and the exact terms you will need on their website:

Honderd jaar Amstelveense bouwdossiers staan digitaal, doorzoekbaar en met scans; losse bestanden en hele dossiers zijn te downloaden met de knoppen op de detailpagina. https://beta.archief.amsterdam/zoeken. Vragen: stadsarchief@amsterdam.nl, 020 251 15 11 (di t/m vr 10.00 tot 12.00 uur).

  • Een deel van de panden is niet openbaar verklaard om veiligheidsredenen. Die komen alleen los met een ingevulde toestemmingsverklaring van de eigenaar of belanghebbende, per mail naar het stadsarchief.
  • Op bouw- en constructietekeningen kan auteursrecht rusten. Voor eigen gebruik is dat geen probleem; voor publicatie wel.

From 1 October 2010

gemeente Amstelveen

From 1 October 2010 the municipality handles it, and its online counter requires a Dutch DigiD login, which I cannot do on your behalf. They aim to send the documents within ten working days.

As I noted it in Dutch, with the links and the exact terms you will need on their website:

Loket Bouwen en Vergunningen, online aan te vragen met DigiD. De gemeente streeft ernaar de stukken binnen tien werkdagen te mailen. Tel. 020 540 49 11. https://loket.amstelveen.nl/aanvraag?schema=registratie_b1100_loketbouwenvergunningen

This counter only supplies the owner in person, after logging in with a Dutch DigiD. I cannot do that for you.

Worth knowing
