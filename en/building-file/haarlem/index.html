Municipality of Haarlem

Where is the building file of your house in Haarlem?

Which archive holds the file depends on the year the permit was granted. Below is where to be for each period, with the date on which I checked it.

This covers Schalkwijk, Haarlem-Noord and Spaarndam-West as well.

Period by period

The counters in Haarlem

Up to 2010 Haarlem is straightforward. The whole holding of the Noord-Hollands Archief is searchable online and a large part of it downloads as a scan, free of charge.

1888 to 2010

Noord-Hollands Archief

Search by address under Uitgebreid zoeken, which is their advanced search. The documents inside a file download one by one. A file holds the application and the permit decision, usually with building drawings and structural drawings in black and white. Questions go to inlichtingen@noord-hollandsarchief.nl.

As I noted it in Dutch, with the links and the exact terms you will need on their website:

Het hele bestand is online doorzoekbaar en een groot deel is als scan te downloaden, gratis. Zoek via Uitgebreid zoeken op adres; de documenten in een dossier zijn afzonderlijk te downloaden. https://noord-hollandsarchief.nl/studiezaal/broninformatie/bouwdossiers-zoeken/haarlem

  • Een dossier bevat de aanvraag en de vergunningverlening, meestal met bouwtekeningen en constructietekeningen in zwart-wit.
  • Vragen: inlichtingen@noord-hollandsarchief.nl

I can look this file up and download it for you.

From 2011

gemeente Haarlem

From 2011 the municipality handles it by email. I have not confirmed that route, so this is the one period here I cannot promise to request for you.

As I noted it in Dutch, with the links and the exact terms you will need on their website:

per mail. https://haarlem.nl/bouwtekeningen-opvragen

For this period I have not yet checked whether the archive also supplies me.

I checked these details on 14 September 2026. Counters and period boundaries change; if something no longer holds, please tell me.
