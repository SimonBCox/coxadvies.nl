Municipality of Veldhoven

Where is the building file of your house in Veldhoven?

Which archive holds the file depends on the year the permit was granted. Below is where to be for each period, with the date on which I checked it.

This covers Veldhoven, Meerveldhoven, Oerle, Zeelst and Zandoerle as well.

Period by period

The counters in Veldhoven

In Veldhoven the older files are with the regional archive in Eindhoven and have not been digitised yet, so expect a longer wait. Recent years come from the municipality by email.

1906 to 1990

Regionaal Historisch Centrum Eindhoven

From 1906 to 1990 the Regionaal Historisch Centrum Eindhoven (RHCe) holds the files, in two series, 1906 to 1945 and 1946 to 1990. Both are on paper and not yet digitised. The oldest series can only be found through cadastral research, not by address. Requests go through the form on the RHCe website; expect a longer processing time.

As I noted it in Dutch, with the links and the exact terms you will need on their website:

(RHCe), in twee reeksen (1906 t/m 1945 en 1946 t/m 1990), allebei analoog en nog niet gedigitaliseerd. De oudste reeks is alleen via kadastraal onderzoek te vinden, niet op adres. Aanvragen met het formulier op https://www.rhc-eindhoven.nl/bouwdossiers-aanvragen, tel. (040) 264 99 40, en reken op een langere doorlooptijd.

Worth knowing

For recent years the municipality of Veldhoven handles it by email at omgevingsloket@veldhoven.nl. The reply comes by email within two weeks. The starting price is € 10,55, with a little more for each additional file.

As I noted it in Dutch, with the links and the exact terms you will need on their website:

de recente jaren: gemeente Veldhoven, per mail bij omgevingsloket@veldhoven.nl. Zet erbij welke bouwplannen of vergunningen u wilt, uw naam met voorletters, adres en een telefoonnummer overdag. Reactie binnen twee weken, per mail. De startprijs is € 10,55, en voor meer dossiers komt er per dossier iets bij.

Bron: https://www.veldhoven.nl/bouwplannen-en-bouwvergunningen-inzien.

I checked these details on 6 September 2026. Counters and period boundaries change; if something no longer holds, please tell me.
